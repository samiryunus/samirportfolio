Rocket Flight Dynamics & Guidance Simulation

I built a full flight simulator for a reusable booster from scratch, covering launch, flip, boostback, entry, and landing. Change the thrust, drag, or guidance law and you can watch exactly how apogee, downrange distance, and touchdown conditions respond.

  • Wrote the 2D equations of motion in MATLAB and Simulink, including gravity, drag, thrust, and fuel mass depletion
  • Built an ISA atmosphere model so density and drag stay realistic from the troposphere into the stratosphere
  • Programmed a gravity turn and pitch command logic that carries the vehicle through every phase of the flight
  • Streamed state data to FlightGear over UDP for a live 3D view of the whole launch and landing

Impact

  • A reusable MATLAB/Simulink tool for Falcon-9-style booster missions with tunable vehicle and guidance parameters.
  • A fast way to test guidance ideas before they ever touch hardware.
  • A first-order pitch control loop that tracks commanded pitch smoothly, without overshoot.
